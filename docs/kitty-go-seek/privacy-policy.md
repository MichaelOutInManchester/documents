# Privacy Policy for Kitty: Go Seek!

**Effective Date:** 9 May 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Michael Out In Manchester Limited (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information in relation to the mobile game *Kitty: Go Seek!* (“the Game”).

The Game is distributed through platforms including the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at:

**Email:** enquiries@michaeloutinmanchester.com  
**Country:** United Kingdom

---

## 2. Information We Collect

At this time, the Game is designed to collect little to no personal information.

The Game currently:

- does not require account creation;
- does not include analytics or advertising SDKs;
- does not include user chat or messaging;
- does not collect precise location data;
- does not collect contact information;
- does not knowingly collect biometric information;
- does not use third-party advertising services;
- does not sell personal information.

The Game may store limited data locally on your device for gameplay purposes, such as:

- save data;
- gameplay progress;
- settings and preferences.

This information is stored locally on the user’s device and is not intentionally transmitted to us.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

The Game may request limited device permissions necessary for functionality, including:

- **Storage/File Access** — used solely for saving and loading gameplay data locally on the device.

The Game does not intentionally access unrelated personal files, photos, contacts, or media.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in relevant jurisdictions). If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected unintentionally, we will take reasonable steps to delete such information.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information may contact us at:

**enquiries@michaeloutinmanchester.com**

---

## 5. International Users

The Game may be made available internationally. By using the Game, you understand that information stored locally on your device may be processed in your country of residence.

Because the Game currently does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to us, cross-border data transfers are limited.
